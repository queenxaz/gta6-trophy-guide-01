    The road to Vice Got a Price!

    One playthrough is enough: there is no difficulty trophy, no online trophy and no mission replay, so the order below is built from the official descriptions — which story chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts, in what order. Around 200 hours in total; the 100% checklist is the long half.

      Save like a trophy hunter

      • Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter — there are 15 manual slots plus one autosave, and no mission replay.
      • Keep one untouched save from just before the final mission.
      • Never test cheats on your trophy save: cheats are expected to switch off trophies and saving for that session.
      • Turn on every progress and collectible notification in the settings, and check the in-game counters (Progress > Stats, Progress > Challenges, Progress > Collections) as you go.

      Sources: Output Lag — GTA 6 Trophy List and GTABase — GTA 6 Trophy Guide.

      Missables & time-sensitive moments

      Almost everything can be done after the credits — the story trophies are unmissable and the side-story finales stay open. Only one trophy carries a real missable risk, plus one time-sensitive race. Both are flagged here, built from the official descriptions.

      What cannot be missed

      All 12 story trophies (the chapter trophies and the named missions) pop as you play. The six side-story finales — Kenton, Billy, Andy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret — stay available after the story, and every free-roam trophy does too. The only thing the game never gives back is a missed moment inside a chapter you have already finished.

      Sources: Output Lag — GTA 6 Trophy List and GTABase — GTA 6 Trophy Guide.

      What is still unconfirmed

      Five hidden trophies — In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin — had no confirmed unlock method at launch. Their guides say so plainly and give only what the official description hints at, rather than guessing. The Fair Game trigger animal is likewise unconfirmed; the guide says so and points at the 15 Target Decks the 100% checklist needs anyway.
